# CepQontag monochrome brand marks

Eight automotive vector marks were adapted from [Sim1Who/CarBrands](https://github.com/Sim1Who/CarBrands), an icon set originally designed by Dan G. Nelson and distributed by that project under the MIT License.

Original marks and brand names are trademarks of their respective owners; they are used only to identify a vehicle's manufacturer and imply no endorsement. All assets are locally stored vector paths with no remote image references, tinted #651FFF in Flutter.

All icons are identification marks, not endorsements by their manufacturers. Brand logos remain their respective owners' trademarks.
